Math 12 — Formula Sheet

Pre-Calculus 12, one page. Print it, keep it, rehearse it.

Functions & transformations

Quadratic$y = a(x - p)^2 + q$
Reciprocal$y = \dfrac{1}{x}$, vertical asymptote $x = 0$
Absolute value$y = |x|$
Translation$y = f(x - h) + k$ shifts right $h$, up $k$
Reflection$y = -f(x)$ across $x$-axis; $y = f(-x)$ across $y$-axis
Stretch$y = a\,f(x)$ vertical by $a$; $y = f(bx)$ horizontal by $\tfrac{1}{b}$
InverseSwap $x$ and $y$, then solve for $y$

Exponential & logarithmic

Definition$y = \log_b x \iff b^y = x$
Product$\log_b (MN) = \log_b M + \log_b N$
Quotient$\log_b \left(\dfrac{M}{N}\right) = \log_b M - \log_b N$
Power$\log_b (M^p) = p \log_b M$
Change of base$\log_b M = \dfrac{\log_a M}{\log_a b}$
Special values$\log_b 1 = 0, \quad \log_b b = 1$

Trigonometry

Pythagorean$\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$
Ratio derived$1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta$
Double angle$\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$
Double angle$\cos 2\theta = \cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta$
Sine law$\dfrac{a}{\sin A} = \dfrac{b}{\sin B} = \dfrac{c}{\sin C}$
Cosine law$c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos C$

Rational functions

Vertical asymptoteWhere the denominator is $0$
Horizontal asymptote$\dfrac{ax^n + \cdots}{bx^m + \cdots}$: $n < m \Rightarrow y = 0$; $n = m \Rightarrow y = \dfrac{a}{b}$
HoleWhen a factor cancels between numerator and denominator
