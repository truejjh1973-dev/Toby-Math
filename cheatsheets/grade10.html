Math 10 — Formula Sheet

Foundations & Pre-Calculus 10, one page. Print it, keep it, rehearse it.

Exponent laws

Product$a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}$
Quotient$\dfrac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$
Power of a power$(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$
Zero exponent$a^0 = 1 \quad (a \neq 0)$
Negative exponent$a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n}$
Fractional exponent$a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a}, \quad a^{m/n} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}$
Scientific notation$a \times 10^k, \quad 1 \le a < 10$

Radicals

Product rule$\sqrt[n]{a} \cdot \sqrt[n]{b} = \sqrt[n]{ab}$
Quotient rule$\dfrac{\sqrt[n]{a}}{\sqrt[n]{b}} = \sqrt[n]{\dfrac{a}{b}}$
Simplify$\sqrt{48} = \sqrt{16 \cdot 3} = 4\sqrt{3}$

Factoring patterns

Common factor$ab + ac = a(b + c)$
Difference of squares$a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$
Perfect square$a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2 = (a \pm b)^2$
Trinomial$x^2 + bx + c = (x + m)(x + n)$, where $mn = c$, $m + n = b$

Linear relations & systems

Slope$m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$
Slope-intercept$y = mx + b$
Point-slope$y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$
Equation of a line$Ax + By = C$
SubstitutionSolve one equation for a variable, then substitute
EliminationAdd or subtract equations to remove a variable
