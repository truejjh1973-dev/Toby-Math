How lessons work

  • Diagnose first. We start with what you can already do, then grow from there.
  • Explain the why. Every rule is introduced with the idea behind it, so it sticks.
  • Practice with feedback. Problems, worked together, with immediate correction.
  • Materials included. The notes and formula sheets from this site make great take-home reading.
