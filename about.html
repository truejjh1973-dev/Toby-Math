About Toby

A student's math journey, in one place — what I study, what I enjoy, and why I built this site.

Hi, I'm Toby

I'm a high school student in British Columbia building a deep interest in mathematics — from contest problem sets to the curriculum behind the grades. This site is where I explain what I'm learning, share the math I find genuinely exciting, and keep a fair record of my progress.

Why build a site at all? Writing an idea down in my own words is the fastest way to find out whether I actually understand it. The site is my exercise book, out in the open.
