About Toby

The person behind the maths — why I study it, teach it, and keep the record public.

Who I am

Hi — I'm Toby, a Grade 11 student in British Columbia. Mathematics is the thread that runs through almost everything I do: the contest problems I solve, the course notes I write for BC grades 10–12, and the teaching I do around all of it.

I got hooked on contest math because the questions reward thinking before speed — a good idea beats a quick hand every time. That shift changed how I see the subject, and it is the thing I most want to pass on.

My current goal is to study mathematics and computer science at university, where deep thinking and clear communication get to meet. This site is part of that work: a portfolio that is honest, dated, and impossible to mistake for marketing.

Math interests

AlgebraNumber theoryCombinatoricsProof-writing

I read widely for fun, but the ideas that pull me back are the ones with a clean invariant or a sneaky bijection at their heart.

Leadership & community

For me, leadership is measured in how much easier the person next to me finds it to explain a solution after we talk. That is why I give public lectures and why I keep teaching competition math to other students instead of coasting on results alone.

"A score shows what I know. Teaching shows what I make possible."

What this site is for

  • For you (if you're learning) — free contest guides, BC course notes, and printable formula sheets.
  • For the record — an honest, dated account of my work, growth, and teaching, for applications ahead.

If you are reading this as part of an application review: everything here is as it was when dated — no retrofits. The same discipline I try to teach is the discipline this site is built on.
