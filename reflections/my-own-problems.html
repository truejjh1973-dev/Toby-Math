My Own Problems

Problems I have designed myself — with my intended solution.

Why design problems at all

Setting a good problem is the honest test of understanding: you have to know not only the path to the answer, but every tempting wrong path a solver might fall into. My designs will be posted here with the intended solution and the story of how I adjusted the difficulty.

Try before you peek. Each problem will come with a "solve it first" note, and the intended solution will sit a page below it.
