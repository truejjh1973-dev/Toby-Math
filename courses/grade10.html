BC Math 10

Foundations & Pre-Calculus 10 — the year where algebra becomes the everyday tool.

The big ideas

Math 10 is the course that turns elementary arithmetic into high-school algebra. Everything after it — quadratics, trigonometry, functions — builds directly on the ideas mastered here.

  • Operations on fractions and rational numbers become fluent and reliable.
  • Exponents and radicals gain rules you can prove, not just memorize.
  • Algebra moves from "solving" to "manipulating" — factoring is the star skill.
  • Two unknowns meeting one condition introduce the idea of a system.

Why these topics matter later

Factoring is the single skill you'll use every single year of high school math — quadratics, rational expressions, and calculus all recycle it. Getting fluent here makes grades 11 and 12 noticeably calmer.

Study tip: practise factoring until the pattern is instant. Twenty five-minute sprints of mixed factoring beat one long session — recall speed is what contests and tests reward.

Check your factoring

1. Factor $x^2 - 16$.

Difference of squares: $x^2 - 16 = (x - 4)(x + 4)$.

2. Factor $x^2 + 7x + 12$.

We need two numbers with sum $7$ and product $12$: $3$ and $4$, so $x^2 + 7x + 12 = (x + 3)(x + 4)$.

3. Simplify $\sqrt{50}$.

$\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \cdot 2} = 5\sqrt{2}$.
