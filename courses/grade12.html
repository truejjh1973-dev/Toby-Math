BC Math 12

Pre-Calculus 12 — the year where algebra grows a vocabulary of functions.

The big ideas

Math 12 is a different kind of year: instead of new arithmetic, you learn a library of functions and the way one function can be stretched, flipped, or shifted into another. It's the closest thing high school offers to a "thinking about thinking" course.

  • Functions and their inverses — every operation undone by another.
  • Transformations — how $y = a\,f(bx - h) + k$ moves a graph around the plane.
  • Exponential and logarithmic functions — growth, decay, and their language.
  • Trigonometric identities — proving and using exact relationships.
  • Rational functions — asymptotes, holes, and their careful graphs.

Check your logarithms

1. $\log_2 8 =$ ?

$\log_2 8$ asks "2 to what power is 8?" Since $2^3 = 8$, the answer is $3$.

2. Which rule simplifies $\log_b (MN)$?

The product rule: $\log_b (MN) = \log_b M + \log_b N$.

Last-year advice: everything in this course is easier when Math 10 factoring and Math 11 quadratics are fluent. If you can see a logarithm equation as "a quadratic wearing a hat", you'll ace most of the year.
