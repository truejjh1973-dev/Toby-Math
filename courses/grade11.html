BC Math 11

Pre-Calculus 11 / Foundations 11 — where algebra develops a face (the quadratic) and triangles gain angles.

The big ideas

Math 11 is where the curriculum leaves "straight-line math" behind. Curves, fractions of polynomials, and triangles measured with angles all arrive in the same year:

  • The quadratic becomes the central object — every shape of parabola, every solving method.
  • Radicals and rational expressions demand real fluency with factoring.
  • Trigonometry moves from right triangles to the sine law and cosine law for any triangle.
  • Inequalities teach you that maths has two sides: equal, and everything on each side of it.

Unit outline

UnitCore skills
QuadraticsVertex forms, factoring, quadratic formula, parabola sketching, applications
RadicalsSimplifying, operations with radicals, radical equations
Rational expressions & equationsSimplifying fractions of polynomials, solving rational equations
TrigonometrySine law, cosine law, right-triangle ratios, word problems
InequalitiesLinear and quadratic inequalities, interval and number-line notation
Financial math (Foundations)Simple and compound interest, and practical rates

Why these topics matter later

The quadratic is the curriculum's cornerstone: in grade 12 you'll transform and combine the functions built on it, and calculus later will measure their slopes. Likewise, sine and cosine laws become the everyday tools of any triangle problem you'll meet in contests.

Study tip: master the quadratic formula and factoring as separate skills. Factoring is faster, but the formula never fails — contests reward having both in your pocket.
