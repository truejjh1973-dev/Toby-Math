Waterloo Math Contests

Canada's best-known contest family, run by the University of Waterloo.

The contests, by grade

Waterloo runs a contest for almost every stage of high school. Each is 60 minutes, with one multiple-choice part and one full-solution part — the second part is why these contests reward clear written reasoning, not just right answers.

ContestTypical gradeStyle
PascalGrade 9Mixed choice + written
CayleyGrade 10Mixed choice + written
FermatGrade 11Mixed choice + written
EuclidGrade 1210 written problems — the famous one

Why the written part matters

In the first section you pick answers. In the second, you write full solutions and earn marks for method and reasoning — a partial solution can still score most of the marks. This is excellent training for university applications, where admissions readers value clear communication.

How to prepare

  • Work from recent years' papers — the question style barely changes year to year.
  • Practise writing solutions, not circling answers. Show every step, then delete the fluff.
  • Time yourself: 60 minutes is shorter than it feels when you write full solutions.
"On the Euclidean section, a clean half-solution beats a messy complete one."
For BC students: Waterloo contests fit naturally alongside the BC curriculum — Pascal maps onto grade 9 math, Cayley onto grade 10, and so on. They are a great first "full-solution" contest to try.
