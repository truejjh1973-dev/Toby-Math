COMC — Canadian Open Mathematics Challenge

Canada's national open contest, run by the Canadian Mathematical Society, and the gateway to the CMO.

What the COMC is

The Canadian Open Mathematics Challenge is the Canadian Mathematical Society's annual open contest. Any Canadian high school student can write it, and a strong result is your clearest route to a Canadian Mathematical Olympiad (CMO) invitation.

FormatDetails
Length3 hours
Part A10 short-answer problems, 4 marks each
Part B4 full-solution problems, 10 marks each
Total80 marks
EligibilityOpen to all Canadian students in high school

Why the written part matters

Part B rewards clear reasoning, not just final answers. A well-structured partial solution earns partial marks — exactly the skill that the CMO and IMO ladder is built on.

How to prepare

  • Work through recent past papers — they are freely available and the style is stable.
  • Practise writing Part B solutions with marks in mind: state the idea, justify it, then finish.
  • Speed up Part A: the first half should be confident, fast arithmetic rather than perseverance.
"COMC tells the story of your reasoning, not just whether you got there."
Official site: cms.math.ca/competitions/comc — registration, dates, and past papers. Most students write through their school.
