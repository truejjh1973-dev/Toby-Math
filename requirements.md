## Requirements Document
### Project Overview
- **Purpose**: Create a personal mathematics research space focused on deep exploration of mathematical concepts.
- **Scope**: Expand beyond contest preparation to include original problem-solving, theoretical explorations, and interdisciplinary connections.

### Features
1. Research hub for open problems and unsolved conjectures
2. Interactive theorem exploration with visualizations
3. Collaborative workspace for mathematical ideas
4. Archives of thought processes and dead ends in research
5. Cross-references to academic papers and resources

### User Stories
- As a researcher, I want to document my explorations of complex problems without easy answers.
- As a learner, I want to see the process of thinking through difficult mathematical concepts.
- As a collaborator, I want to contribute ideas and solutions to open problems.

### Acceptance Criteria
- All content is dated and verifiable
- No focus on contest scores or college applications
- Emphasis on research process over results
