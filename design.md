## Design Document
### Architecture
- **Single-page application** structure with client-side routing
- **Modular components**: Research notes, problem solvers, visualization tools
- **Data layer**: JSON-based storage for research progress and annotations

### Technical Implementation
- **Frontend**: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS with focus on accessibility
- **Search system**: Enhanced to handle mathematical notation and concepts
- **Theme**: Dark mode optimized for long reading sessions
- **Interactivity**: Live rendering of mathematical expressions and diagrams

### UI/UX Considerations
- Focus on readability for dense mathematical content
- Support for LaTeX math notation
- Visual hierarchy emphasizing thought process over final answers
- Offline-readable format for research notes
